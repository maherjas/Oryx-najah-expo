// Quick answers

Questions we hear a lot

Do I need coding experience?

Not to start. The programmes build up gradually, so ask our team how the first modules work.

What is the difference between the BSc and MSc?

The BSc builds your foundations from the beginning. The MSc is for graduates and professionals who want to specialise.

Who awards the degree?

Both the BSc (Hons) Cyber Security and the MSc Cyber Security are awarded by Liverpool John Moores University (LJMU), delivered at Oryx University.

Is my data collected in these games?

Your challenge answers, scores and certificate are processed entirely inside your browser. No personal information leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to submit your score to the leaderboard. If you do submit, only your chosen display name and score are stored in a private Google Sheet accessible to event organisers, and the data is deleted within 30 days of the Expo.

What about the visitor counter?

The counter at the bottom of the page shows a running total of page loads on this demonstration site. It is not a count of unique visitors and it does not collect any information about you.
